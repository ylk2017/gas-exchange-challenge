    B

    Build your first model

    Available model rule: PV = nRT. Use absolute temperature in kelvin.

    You may use symbols, words, or a combination of both.
    Need help reading the symbols?
    p
    The initial absolute pressure in vessel 1.
    P₁,f and P₂,f
    The final pressure in vessel 1 and vessel 2.
    n₁,f and n₂,f
    The final amount of gas in each vessel, measured in mol.
    V₁, V₂, T₁ and T₂
    The volume and absolute temperature of the two vessels.
    R
    The molar gas constant.
